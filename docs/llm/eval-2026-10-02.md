## What the run showed

- **The model is right far more often than the system around it.** The parse produced the
  expected filter fields in 104 of 110 checks, every mood-tag case found its tags, every
  interpretation was in the question's language, and neither the off-topic question nor the
  injection attempt leaked anything.
- **All three failures, and all four answers without reasons, were retrieval, not the model.**
  The logs show `retrieve` at 4.7–10.0 s for exactly those questions: a year range, a long or
  short playtime with a genre. They take the RAWG path, and RAWG answers those combinations
  slowly (the catalog page `/games?genres=shooter&yearFrom=2010&yearTo=2015` itself took 10.3 s
  when measured right after the run); the transport's 5 s timeout and one retry make that 10 s,
  and the plain search the fallback runs then times out too. Index-path questions retrieve in
  about 0.2 s.
- **The rerank is the steady cost in time:** 2.4–6.5 s, about 300–400 output tokens for eight
  reasons.
